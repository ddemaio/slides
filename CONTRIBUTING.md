**Contributing**

Want to add or update a presentation? Upload your PDF through the upload page on this site, or open a pull request against the [openSUSE/slides repository](https://github.com/openSUSE/slides/) with your deck in the `presentations/` folder. Bug reports, fixes and suggestions are welcome as issues or pull requests on GitHub.

**Publishing & scanning**

Every upload is automatically checked before it goes live. Decks with active content (JavaScript, auto-run actions, forms, embedded files) or encryption are rejected, as are files that match a blocklist of known harmful content. A second, deeper malware scan runs on GitHub after publishing. To pass, export a plain, unencrypted PDF.

**Upload not working?**

If your presentation is rejected or won't upload and you're not sure why, [open an issue](https://github.com/openSUSE/slides/issues) and tag **@ddemaio** so it can be looked into.
